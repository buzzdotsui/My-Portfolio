# Personal Portfolio Evolution — Testimony Owolabi

## Context

I already have an existing personal portfolio website.

**Do NOT rebuild the website from scratch.**

First, inspect and understand the existing codebase, architecture, components, design system, animations, responsiveness, assets, content structure, and current user experience.

Then evolve the existing portfolio into a significantly stronger personal brand website.

The goal is to preserve what is already good while redesigning, restructuring, and rewriting what is necessary.

---

# 1. Core Identity

The website should now represent me primarily as:

> **Full-Stack Software Engineer building intelligent systems for industry.**

I am a Materials and Metallurgical Engineering student at the Federal University of Technology Akure (FUTA), while simultaneously working as a full-stack software engineer.

My differentiation is the intersection of:

**Software Engineering × Materials Engineering × Intelligent Systems**

I am building **Metabotics**, a technology initiative focused on applying software, automation, data, and intelligent systems to materials, manufacturing, and industrial processes.

Do NOT position me primarily as:

* DevSecOps Engineer
* Cybersecurity Engineer
* Web3 Developer

Those are parts of my technical background and can remain in the appropriate sections, but they should no longer define my primary identity.

My primary identity is:

> **Full-Stack Software Engineer + Materials/Metallurgical Engineering student building intelligent industrial systems.**
